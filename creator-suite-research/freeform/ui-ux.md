## UX principles to preserve in our version (design choices, not observations)
- Keep primary workflow reachable within three clicks.
- Full keyboard operability and screen-reader labels.
- Native-feeling behaviour on Windows, Linux and macOS (own design, not a copy of Apple visuals or trade dress).
